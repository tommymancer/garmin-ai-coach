# Training plan

block_start: 2026-01-06
block_weeks: 4

A repeating 4-week block. Goal: lose weight while rotating bike / swim / run,
growing chronic load without spikes.

## Block progression

| Week | Sessions | Target weekly load | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | ~330 | consolidation |
| 2 | 5 | ~370 | +10% |
| 3 | 5-6 | ~410 | block peak |
| 4 | 4 | ~240 | deload (not optional) |

## Weekly template

- Mon — rest, or 30min easy swim
- Tue — Run easy 40-45min, zone 2
- Wed — Swim 35-40min, technique + aerobic
- Thu — QUALITY (1x/week): run 10min warmup + 6x2min hard / 2min easy + 10min cooldown
        or bike tempo 50min
- Fri — rest / long walk
- Sat — LONG BIKE 75-100min, zone 2 — the most valuable session of the week
- Sun — Run easy 30-40min, or swim 30min

## Guardrails

- 80/20: exactly ONE hard session per week; everything else below the zone-2 ceiling.
- Keep ACWR between 0.8 and 1.3; the +10%/week progression exists to stay in that band.
- Deload every 4th week. Non-negotiable.
- The Saturday long ride is the highest-value session (big calorie burn, low impact,
  easy to recover from). If something must be cut, don't cut that.
- Weigh in 2-3 mornings a week so the trend is readable.

## How to use this in feedback

Compare what was actually done against the template and the week's load target.
If a session is skipped or shortened, suggest how to make it up without spiking
ACWR. If they're ahead of the load target, hold them back. The plan is a guide,
not a cage — adapt to how they feel and what the data says.
